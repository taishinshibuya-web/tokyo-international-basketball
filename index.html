About TIB

Who We Are

Tokyo International Ballers (TIB) is an international basketball community based in Tokyo, Japan, established in June 2024.

We bring together people from all over the world — students, locals, and everyone in between — to connect through the universal language of basketball.

Our Mission

"Cultural Exchange Through Basketball"

The management members who all have experienced sports communication outside of Japan provide you with a great opportunity to make friends and exchange cultures through basketball in Tokyo.

Why TIB?

What Makes Us Unique

Three things that set our community apart.

Welcome to Both Japanese & International Students

We welcome not just international students from all over the world, but also Japanese students who want to improve their English or simply want to have fun.

Open to Everyone of All Abilities & Genders

We offer sessions for beginners and advanced players of all genders. Whether it's your first time touching a basketball — you're welcome here.

No Membership Fee

We do not charge a membership fee. We only charge a court fee for each session, and the price depends on the location and number of participants.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. If you can't find what you're looking for, reach out on Instagram.

How many players are in each session? ▾
Depends on the session, but roughly 10–20 players typically join each game.
How often are the sessions? ▾
Session info & updates will be posted on Instagram and on this website. Follow @tib_basketball to stay in the loop.
Where are the players from? ▾
Everywhere! Our community includes players from Japan, Korea, the US, various European countries, and beyond. That diversity is what makes TIB special.
What do I need to bring for sessions? ▾
Bring sports wear, your own drinks, and indoor shoes. No need to bring your own basketball — we've got that covered!
Is there a membership fee? ▾
No membership fee! We only charge a court fee per session, which varies depending on the location and number of participants.
I've never played basketball before. Can I still join? ▾
Absolutely! In fact, 40% of our participants have never played basketball before. TIB is a welcoming, inclusive space for all skill levels. Everyone starts somewhere!
Can Japanese students join even though the sessions are in English? ▾
Of course! We warmly welcome Japanese students who want to practice their English, meet international friends, or simply enjoy basketball in a fun, multicultural environment.
How do I sign up for a session? ▾
You can sign up via the Luma link posted on our Instagram, or send us a DM at @tib_basketball. Head to the Schedule & Sign Up page for full details.
